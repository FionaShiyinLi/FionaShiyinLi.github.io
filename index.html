Shiyin (Fiona) Li

PhD candidate in biostatistics, University of Arizona

I work on evidence synthesis: how to combine results from separate studies, and how to report the uncertainty of the combined answer when only a few studies exist.

My dissertation builds empirical prior distributions for between-study heterogeneity in Bayesian meta-analysis from two decades of Cochrane reviews. I also test what language models can do for systematic reviews, and I analyze trials and meta-analyses with clinical collaborators.

Research

Bayesian meta-analysis with few studies

Most meta-analyses contain only a few studies, too few to estimate between-study heterogeneity well from the data alone. I develop empirical priors for it, separately for odds ratios, risk ratios, risk differences, and standardized mean differences, using Cochrane reviews published from 2003 to 2025. Each prior is judged by how well it predicts study results it has not seen, and by simulation.

Dissertation research, in progress.

How Cochrane reviews are produced and structured

I build databases of Cochrane reviews and use them to describe the evidence base itself. In a study of review lifecycles, 71.9% of 8,137 protocols went on to become full reviews, after a median of 25.7 months.

Related: Lifecycles of Cochrane systematic reviews (2003–2024)

Language models in evidence synthesis

Systematic reviews depend on repeated expert judgment, which makes them a demanding test for language models. I have compared fine-tuned biomedical language models with a prompted general-purpose model for classifying outcome types, used a language model to flag overlooked trial reports for human reassessment in a cancer-screening evidence base, and measured how figure format affects the reconstruction of Kaplan–Meier data.

Related: cancer-screening evidence audit, Kaplan–Meier figure format, outcome classification (in press)

Clinical collaborations

I work with clinical researchers on randomized trials and systematic reviews, using mixed-effects models, mediation analysis, and meta-analysis. Published work includes a meta-analysis of customized titanium mesh for alveolar bone regeneration.

Related: Patient-specific customized titanium mesh
